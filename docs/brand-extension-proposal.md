# Brand extension for the web — for approval

Prospectus §2.2 invites a proposal for extending the Momentum brand online. Everything below is already implemented and can be adjusted.

## Colour

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — Momentum teal | `#33CBCC` | Buttons, highlights, the rising line, icons |
| Teal ink (accessible) | `#0A7475` | Teal **text** and links. #33CBCC on white is too low-contrast for text (WCAG 2.2 AA), so a deeper teal from the same hue is used |
| Secondary — charcoal | `#423E3B` | Body text |
| Deep charcoal | `#22201E` | Dark sections, footer, report cards |
| Warm stone | `#FAF9F7` / `#F3F1EE` | Page background and panels: a warmer, more personal feel than pure white |
| Teal tints | `#EFFBFB` / `#D3F4F4` | Soft highlight backgrounds |

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** (open font licence, self-hosted): a confident, modern geometric sans with friendly rounded details. Extra-bold weights for headlines echo the weight of the logo's M; regular and medium weights keep body copy easy to read. The logo wordmark keeps its italic style.

## Motion — "forward movement and growth"

- **The rising line.** The chart line from the logo draws itself across hero sections, page headers and call-to-action panels.
- **Growth reveals.** Chart bars grow upward, numbers count up, and content rises gently into view on scroll.
- **Restraint.** Animation never blocks reading. Everything is disabled automatically for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

## Photography (to commission)

- Real team photos in the office, natural light, warm and approachable, not stock "handshake" imagery.
- Clients' businesses in action (a construction site, studio or office) for case studies, with permission.
- Ben at work for the About page and social posts, matching his existing Instagram and TikTok style.
- Consistent treatment: warm white balance, with teal accents where natural.

## Voice

Plain English, short sentences, written to directors ("you"), not to accountants. Lead with outcomes ("no surprises", "know your numbers") and back them with specifics ("filed within three months").
