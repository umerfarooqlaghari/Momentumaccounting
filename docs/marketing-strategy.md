# Post-launch marketing strategy (prospectus §7)

> Draft for discussion. Budget tiers use a transparent model. Replace the assumption rows with the agency's own benchmarks before sending to the practice.

## Approach

1. **Local SEO (always on).** Google Business Profile with weekly posts and a review drive. Local pages for Ashford, Staines, Sunbury, Egham and Feltham. Service and sector pages. Two articles a month answering director questions.
2. **Google Search Ads.** High-intent searches ("accountant for limited company Surrey", "switch accountant", "accountants Ashford"). Traffic goes to `/lp/switch-accountant` and service pages, optimised on the lead conversion.
3. **Paid social (Meta + LinkedIn).** Retarget site visitors with the quarterly report and the health-check quiz. Run lookalike audiences of directors on LinkedIn.
4. **Founder content (Instagram, TikTok).** Ben's existing videos link to the quiz and calculator with UTM-tagged links (e.g. `?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=bio`). Best posts are embedded on the site (Site settings → Featured social posts) and turned into blog articles.
5. **Nurture.** Opted-in prospects receive the four-email sequence until they book.

## Budget model

| Input | Formula |
|---|---|
| Clicks | Monthly ad spend ÷ average cost per click |
| Leads | Clicks × landing-page conversion rate |
| Cost per lead | Ad spend ÷ leads |
| New clients | Leads × lead-to-client rate |
| First-year value | New clients × average monthly fee × 12 |
